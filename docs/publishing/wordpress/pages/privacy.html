FavoritePod privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Covers FavoritePod version 1.0.0

FavoritePod is a Chrome extension provided by Rocky Road Studio. This policy explains how the extension handles your data and how to control it. Privacy questions can be sent to rockyroadstudio@outlook.com.

Your library is stored in your Chrome profile. If you choose Google Drive sync, library data and previews also go directly to your Google Drive. The extension has no Rocky Road Studio server that receives your library, and includes no advertising, analytics, or automatic error-reporting service.

1. Information the extension handles

  • Your library: saved page titles and URLs, local-file paths, folder names and relationships, ordering, website icons, preview images, and edits you make. Bookmark and backup files you choose to import are read to add their contents to your library.
  • Page previews: when you use the toolbar to save a page or refresh a preview, FavoritePod can capture the visible page and compress it into a thumbnail. You can also upload an image in the favorite editor. A screenshot may include personal communications, health or financial information, or other private content visible at capture time. Preview images are not analyzed to infer those details. Review the page before capturing, or remove its preview.
  • Local settings and recovery data: appearance and layout preferences, hidden shortcuts, recent-item dismissals, device-specific file path mappings, undo/recovery information, and up to three local library snapshots. The extension also keeps sync status, error messages, and up to 20 recent sync-history entries locally.
  • Optional frequently visited sites: with your Top sites permission, FavoritePod reads Chrome's selected site titles and URLs to display shortcuts. It does not request the History permission or build a separate browsing-history log. Shortcuts and hiding preferences stay local unless you explicitly save a shortcut as a favorite.
  • Optional Google Drive connection: Google provides the account email address and permission identifier so FavoritePod can identify the connected account and prevent unintended account switching. Chrome's identity API supplies OAuth access tokens for authorized requests. FavoritePod does not receive your Google password or store access tokens in its library database or backup exports.
  • Sync metadata: generated device/profile identifiers, record identifiers, modification clocks, revisions, file identifiers, and deletion markers let your connected profiles synchronize reliably. Drive storage information is used to display available capacity and sync problems.

2. Use, storage, and sharing

FavoritePod uses this information to save, organize, find, display, open, import, export, recover, and optionally synchronize your favorites. The local library and settings are held in browser storage associated with the extension.

Google Drive sync is optional. If you connect, your saved library, preview assets, icons included in saved records, and sync metadata are sent directly from the extension to Google over HTTPS. They are stored in FavoritePod's hidden application-data folder in your Drive and downloaded to your other connected profiles. The requested scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata; it does not grant access to all your Drive documents.

Original local files are not uploaded or mirrored by sync. Their saved paths and captured preview images can be included in synchronized records and exported backups. Device-specific path-remapping settings remain local.

Website icons and destinations: FavoritePod can use Chrome's favicon service and request a site-provided icon URL. An icon provider may receive ordinary network request information, such as your IP address. When you open a saved website or external link, that destination applies its own privacy practices.

Exports: ZIP backups and HTML exports are saved where you choose. A ZIP can include previews and a generated exporting-device identifier. Anyone with access to a backup can read its contents. Sharing an export is your choice.

Rocky Road Studio does not sell your library data or use it for advertising, data brokerage, creditworthiness, lending, or AI model training. Library data is not sent to a developer backend. Human access by the developer is limited to information you deliberately send for support.

FavoritePod's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google handles information under its own privacy policy.

3. Your controls and deletion

You can edit or delete favorites, remove or replace their previews, export your library, and turn optional features on or off in Settings. Local-file access is controlled separately in Chrome's extension details.

Deleting an item is not immediate erasure of every copy. Deletion markers and previously saved record fields may remain for synchronization and undo. Recovery snapshots and previously exported files can also retain older data. Unreferenced Drive preview assets are eligible for cleanup only after at least 90 days and a later successful cleanup run; this is not a guaranteed erasure deadline. There is no fixed automatic purge deadline for all deleted-record metadata.

Disconnecting Drive stops sync and clears the remembered account and sync bookkeeping in that Chrome profile. It leaves your local library and existing Drive data in place, and does not revoke the Google account-wide authorization. Other connected profiles can continue syncing.

To remove your data more completely:

  1. Export anything you want to keep, then disconnect FavoritePod in every connected profile so an active device cannot recreate Drive data.
  2. Use Google Drive's app-management controls to delete FavoritePod's hidden app data. Revoke FavoritePod's access separately in your Google Account's third-party connections if you no longer want it authorized.
  3. Remove FavoritePod from each Chrome profile to remove that profile's extension storage, including local recovery copies. Removing it from one profile does not erase another profile's library.
  4. Delete any ZIP/HTML backups and other copies you made separately.

Google controls retention of data in its services. A later reconnection can upload a remaining local library again. We cannot erase data directly from your device, your Drive, or backups you control; contact us if you need help with the steps.

4. Security

Google authentication is handled by Google and Chrome. Authorized Drive requests use HTTPS. FavoritePod uses browser-managed storage locally and does not add a separate encryption layer to library records or password-protect exported backups. Protect access to your Chrome profile, device, Google account, and exported files.

5. Support and external services

If you email Rocky Road Studio, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond, troubleshoot the reported issue, and keep necessary support records. Messages are processed through our email provider, Microsoft Outlook. Please avoid sending passwords, tokens, private screenshots, or a full library unless you specifically intend to share it.

Support correspondence is retained for responding to and following up on your request and for necessary recordkeeping. You can request access, correction, or deletion of the support information you supplied by emailing us; applicable legal obligations may require some information to be retained.

The product website and listing may link to Buy Me a Coffee for voluntary contributions. Following that link opens a separate service. Buy Me a Coffee and its payment providers handle payment information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may receive supporter details that the service makes available to creators, such as a supporter name, message, and contribution details. The extension does not process payments or send your bookmark library to Buy Me a Coffee. Contributions do not unlock features.

This policy covers FavoritePod and related support. Browsing the Rocky Road Studio website is also subject to the website's own published privacy information and hosting practices.
