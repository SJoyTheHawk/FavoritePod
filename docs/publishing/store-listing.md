## Long description

```text
Your favorite websites and local tools, together in one visual home.

FavoritePod helps you save pages, recognize them by their previews, and find them again without digging through tabs. Organize websites and local file links into folders, browse your collection in a grid or list, and optionally sync your library with your own Google Drive.

SAVE AND FIND
• Save the current page from the Chrome toolbar, with its title, address, and a preview when capture is available.
• Refresh or remove a saved preview, or upload an image from the favorite editor.
• Search your library by name or address.
• Switch between preview cards and a compact list, with adjustable bookmark sizes and light, dark, or system appearance.

ORGANIZE YOUR WAY
• Create folders and subfolders for work, learning, projects, and everyday browsing.
• Drag favorites to reorder them or move them between folders.
• Move multiple favorites together and use undo/redo for library edits.
• Enable the optional right-click “Save to FavoritePod” menu for pages and links.

KEEP LOCAL TOOLS CLOSE
Save links to local HTML tools and files alongside websites. Enable Chrome’s “Allow access to file URLs” setting to use local-file features. If a folder lives at a different path on another computer, configure a device-specific path remap.

SYNC WHEN YOU WANT
Saving, organizing, searching, and exporting work without an account. Connect Google Drive when you want your library on other Chrome profiles or computers. FavoritePod uses its own hidden app-data folder in your Drive. Your library stays available locally while offline; websites still need their own internet access.

Local files themselves are not uploaded or copied to another computer. Their saved addresses and preview images can sync, and the original files must exist on each computer where you open them. Disconnecting Drive stops sync in the current profile while keeping your local library and existing Drive data.

IMPORT AND EXPORT
• Export a FavoritePod ZIP backup with folders, favorites, and previews.
• Import a FavoritePod backup with options to copy, update, or replace.
• Import Chrome bookmarks from an HTML export.
• Export an HTML bookmarks file for Chrome or another compatible browser.

OPTIONAL FREQUENTLY VISITED SHORTCUTS
Grant Top sites access to show shortcuts selected and ordered by Chrome. Hide individual shortcuts or the whole section. FavoritePod does not request Chrome’s History permission; shortcuts stay local unless you explicitly save them as favorites.

YOUR DATA
FavoritePod stores saved titles, URLs, folders, preview images, and preferences in your Chrome profile. Previews capture visible page content and may include personal information shown on the page. Optional Drive sync transfers your library, previews, and sync metadata directly to Google; Google sign-in also supplies account information used to identify the connected account. Favicons may be fetched from the site’s icon provider.

The extension includes no advertising, analytics, or automatic error-reporting service, and Rocky Road Studio does not receive your library through an extension backend. Read the FavoritePod privacy policy linked on this listing for data handling and deletion details.

HELP AND OPTIONAL SUPPORT
Questions or feedback: rockyroadstudio@outlook.com
Website: https://rockyroad.studio

Enjoying FavoritePod? You can optionally support its development:
https://buymeacoffee.com/RockyRoadStudio
Contributions are voluntary and do not unlock additional features.
```
